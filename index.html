Brightcart: where the profit really comes from

Two years of simulated orders (Jan 2024 to Dec 2025): 4,000 customers, 40 products and 19,108 orders. Eight SQL questions on revenue, margin, discounting, customer value and retention.

Simulated data. The dataset was generated by a seeded Python script in the project repo. The shop and its numbers are made up, so the findings show the analysis method and do not describe a real business.

What the numbers show. Region and acquisition channel show little difference (regional margins sit between 24.5% and 25.1%), so the analysis focuses on product, discount and customer behaviour.

Method. Four CSV tables loaded into SQLite and joined into one line-item table. Validation checks: no orphan order lines, no orders before signup, unique order IDs. Queries use CTEs, LAG, NTILE and cohort date arithmetic. See the sql/ folder in the repo.
